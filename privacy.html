Privacy policy

Updated October 6, 2026.

Subnox organizes subscriptions and recurring expenses. You do not need to create an app account.

Local records

Service names, amounts, dates, categories, notes and payment-method labels are stored on your device. Subnox does not send your subscription list to its own server or connect to bank accounts. Payment-method labels are for organization only: do not enter passwords, security codes or full card numbers in notes.

Premium purchases

The App Store or Google Play processes purchases. RevenueCat validates purchases, restores access and handles status changes such as refunds. It processes purchase records, transaction identifiers, an app user identifier and technical information needed for the integration. Subnox does not receive your full store payment-card number. The subscriptions you organize locally are separate from your Premium purchase history and are not sent to RevenueCat by this integration.

Exchange rates

The app requests BRL/USD rates from Frankfurter. Requests contain the currency pair, not your subscription list. The provider may receive connection information such as your IP address.

Reminders

If you enable reminders and allow notifications, reminders are scheduled locally on your device. You can change app preferences or revoke the operating system's notification permission. Delivery depends on your device's battery and notification settings.

Exports and backups

JSON and CSV exports contain the selected records and are not independently encrypted. You choose where to share them; that destination's privacy rules also apply. Operating system backups may include app data depending on your device settings.

Advertising

This version has no ads or advertising-tracking integration.

Deletion and requests

You can delete records inside the app. Uninstalling or clearing app data may remove local records; exported copies and backups need to be deleted separately. Uninstalling does not remove purchase history maintained by the store or cancel a subscription. For questions or requests to delete information processed for support or purchases, contact joaovitoruser@gmail.com. Requests are verified and may be subject to retention obligations of the stores and service providers.
