The app

Subnox stores subscription records on your device. You enter service names, amounts and dates. The app does not automatically discover subscriptions, access bank accounts or cancel third-party services.

Free and Pro plans

You can manage up to four records for free. Starting with version 1.1, Pro unlocks unlimited records through a one-year subscription that renews automatically. The store displays the applicable price, currency and taxes before you confirm. Previous lifetime purchases and courtesy licenses retain access as confirmed by RevenueCat.

Payment and cancellation

The App Store or Google Play processes payment through your store account. Cancel renewal in your store's subscription settings before the next billing period. Access remains available while your paid period is active. Uninstalling the app does not cancel your subscription. Refund requests follow the responsible store's rules.

Restoring and your records

Restore purchases with the same store account on the same platform. A purchase does not automatically move local records to another device. Export and keep backups to migrate your records. If Premium expires, existing records are not deleted; adding records above the free limit requires Pro access.

Reminders and calculations

Reminders are optional and depend on your device permissions and settings. Monthly and yearly totals are equivalents calculated from the amounts you enter, rather than a history of actual payments or a guarantee of future charges. Confirm prices and dates with the service provider.
